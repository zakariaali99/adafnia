# Adafnia: homepage mockup v2 (2026-10-15)

v1 was rejected as "bad and complicated": too technical, too plain, and the side line looked broken. v2 follows the owner's choice of **Rivulis + Wavin** as references, in a warm and friendly direction.

- Serve this folder (`python3 -m http.server 8791`). Add `?intro=1` to replay the intro, `?nointro` to skip it.
- One strong colour: Adafnia blue `#2860B0`. The water cyan is used only in the wave.
- Font: Almarai. Rounded cards (20px), pill buttons, soft shadows.
- Hero: full-width aerial photo of the factory with a wave edge (from Wavin).
- Sections: welcome + 4 stats · solution photo tiles (from Rivulis) · product grid + catalogue card (from Wavin) · about split · quality badges · news · blue contact block with a wave and 4 sales-centre cards.
- No spec tables, technical drawings or section numbers on the homepage. The spec selector moves to the products page.
- Photos come from the company profile (1024 px max). A real photo shoot is needed for a sharp full-width hero.
